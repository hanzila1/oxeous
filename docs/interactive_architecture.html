Figure 1. End-to-end architectural pipeline of the Oxeous autonomous Earth observation compliance agent. The system executes a strictly sequential, four-tier pipeline for EUDR (Regulation EU 2023/1115) compliance verification: (1) Ingest Layer: Producer farm polygons and declared commodity parameters are submitted via GeoJSON boundaries. (2) Satellite Orchestration Layer: A parallel Earth Engine query evaluates six authoritative datasets (Hansen GFC, Nature Trace, Forest Typology, WRI Loss Drivers, FDP Commodity Maps, and UNEP-WCMC WDPA) to verify physical forest cover changes post-December 31, 2020. (3) Intelligence Layer: Large-scale plots undergo dynamic scale optimization (_compute_scale) before passing to an algorithmic risk scoring engine that enforces hard zero-deforestation cutoffs and legal boundary constraints; Gemini 2.0 Flash then generates evidence-grounded legal memorandums. (4) Delivery Layer: Findings are streamed in real time via Server-Sent Events (SSE) to an interactive MapLibre WebGL canvas and committed as immutable, court-ready JSON audit trajectories (trajectories/req_*.json) meeting EUDR Article 9 requirements.
