(Axis 2 - Temporal Trajectory): Evaluates canopy evolution across $T_0$ (2000 historical baseline), $T_{\text{cutoff}}$ (December 31, 2020 statutory benchmark), and $T_{\text{current}}$ (2021–2025 post-cutoff disturbances). (Axis 3 - Statutory Legal Ontology): Translates EUDR statutory provisions into spatial map algebra, distinguishing illegal forest conversion (Article 3(a)) from exempt cyclical agricultural replanting (Article 2(5)) and sustainable commercial silviculture (Article 2(3)/2(7)). A 6-pixel connected-component sieve enforces the FAO $\ge 0.5\text{ ha}$ Minimum Mapping Unit (Article 2(4)). Remediation: Under the May 2026 European Commission Plot Segregation Protocol, localized violations are vectorized and subtracted ($P_{\text{export}} = P_{\text{declared}} \setminus \mathcal{V}_{3(a)}$), generating an audit-admissible Due Diligence Statement (DDS) for EU Single Window customs clearance under TARIC Box 44 document code C716 with SHA-256 cryptographic provenance.
