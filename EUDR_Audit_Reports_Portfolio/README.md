## 🔬 Scientific Multi-Sensor Framework (IEEE Standards)

Each audit cross-references 7 independent Earth observation and geospatial collections:
1. **Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A (10m)** — Multi-spectral surface reflectance & NDVI phenology trajectories.
2. **Hansen Global Forest Change v1.13 (30m)** — Annual forest disturbance ($lossyear$) & canopy density baseline.
3. **Google Forest Data Partnership (10m, model_2026a)** — CNN deep-learning commodity canopy probability heatmaps with authentic GEE palettes.
4. **Nature Trace Natural Forests 2020 (10m)** — Tropical natural forest ecosystem baseline (31 Dec 2020).
5. **Forest Typology ForTy 2020 (10m)** — Global 10m agricultural and agroforestry land-use classification.
6. **WRI Drivers of Loss 2025 (30m)** — Spatial disturbance attribution modeling.
7. **UNEP-WCMC WDPA** — Vector spatial intersection against gazetted IUCN category I–VI national parks and reserves.
